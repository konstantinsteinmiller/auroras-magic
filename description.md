# Auroras Magic

Store copy for the portal listings (CrazyGames, Poki, Playgama, GamePix,
GameMonetize, GameDistribution, Yandex, itch.io, Wavedash). Counts are
characters, measured against each portal's usual limit.

## Short description (123 / 150 chars)

Draw runes to duel a dark unicorn. Sketch fire, wind, ice and earth, stack
three, and cast the combo before your rival lands hers.

## Description (476 / 500 chars)

A gesture-driven magic duel. Draw a triangle for fire, a wave for wind, a Z for
ice, a square for earth. Rough shapes are fine: the rune reader is built to
forgive. Stack up to three runes and cast them together. Every combination is
a different spell, from a quick Fire Bolt to a three-rune Prism Nova. Watch
your rival's rune form above her horn and answer with a barrier. The sky keeps
score: a rainbow as you win, rain as you lose.

## How to play

You are Aurora, the white unicorn on the left. Your rival on the right is
casting too, and she does not wait for you.

1. **Draw a rune anywhere on the screen.** On a phone held upright, the big
   pad under the arena is yours to draw on. There are four shapes, each one
   continuous stroke:

   | Shape | Rune |
   | :-- | :-- |
   | Triangle | **Fire** |
   | Wavy line | **Wind** |
   | **Z** zig-zag | **Ice** |
   | Square | **Earth** |

   Direction and starting corner do not matter, and neither does neatness.
2. **Stack up to three runes.** They fill the slots under your health bar.
3. **Cast.** All stored runes fire as one combined spell. The CAST button
   always shows the name of the spell you have loaded.
4. **Defend when you must.** A square on its own raises an Earth Wall that
   stops everything. Two waves make a Wind Wall against projectiles. Two Zs
   plant an Ice Pillar that eats one hit. Your rival's forming rune is your
   tell.
5. **Climb the ladder.** Each win brings a new rival with her own element. The
   rune shown under her health bar hits her for ×1.7. Spend the coins you win
   on element ranks (+12% damage each) on the result screen.

**Tip:** repeating an element is the strong opening. Two triangles make Fire
Storm; three make Fire Rain.

## Controls

| Action | Desktop | Touch |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Draw a rune | Click and drag anywhere | Drag anywhere |
| Cast | **Space**, **Enter** or **E**, or the CAST button | Tap CAST |
| Sound on / off | **M**, or the note button | Tap the note button |
| Options (language, volume) | The gear button | Tap the gear button |
| Duel again | **Space**, or click the result panel | Tap the result panel |

The game opens straight into the duel and teaches the core gesture in three
short beats. No menu, no loading screen.
